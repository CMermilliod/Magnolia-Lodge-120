Magnolia Lodge #120

About Magnolia Lodge #120

Magnolia Lodge #120 is a lodge of Free and Accepted Masons dedicated to brotherly love, relief, and truth.

Our members support the community through charitable activities, scholarship programs, blood drives, and service projects.

We strive to make good men better and provide opportunities for personal growth, leadership, friendship, and community service.

What is Freemasonry?

Freemasonry is one of the world's oldest charitable and community-focused organizations. Freemasons are ordinary men from all walks of life who share values such as integrity, kindness, personal responsibility, and service to others. Through charitable giving, volunteer work, scholarship programs, support for local causes, and fundraising for those in need, Freemasons help strengthen the communities where they live and work. While their traditions and history are unique, their mission is simple: to make good men better and to leave their communities better than they found them.
